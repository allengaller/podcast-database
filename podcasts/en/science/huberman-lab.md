# Huberman Lab

## Summary

Science and health podcast by Stanford neuroscientist Andrew Huberman, translating neuroscience and biology into actionable protocols for sleep, focus, fitness, and longevity. One of the biggest science podcasts of the 2020s.

## Host

- **Andrew Huberman** — Professor of neurobiology at Stanford; became a massive wellness/science influencer via the show.

## Format

- **Category**: Science / health
- **Format**: Solo deep-dives + expert guest episodes
- **Cadence**: Weekly
- **Episode length**: Often 2–3+ hours
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Health-conscious general listeners, biohackers, athletes, and professionals seeking science-backed self-improvement.

## Notable episodes

- Multi-hour "toolkits" on sleep, dopamine, cold exposure, and focus.
- Expert guest episodes on hormones, fitness, and longevity.

## Reach & assessment

- Among the most-listened science/health podcasts globally; huge YouTube reach.
- Business model: high-volume sponsorships + supplement partnerships.
