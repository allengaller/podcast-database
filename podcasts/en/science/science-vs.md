# Science Vs

## Summary

A science podcast that pits facts against popular ideas and fads — diets, wellness trends, drugs, and controversies — to see what the evidence actually says. Fast-paced, witty, and firmly evidence-based.

## Host

- **Wendy Zukerman** — Science journalist; energetic, skeptical host.

## Format

- **Category**: Science / skepticism
- **Format**: Host + expert interviews, narrative-edited
- **Cadence**: Seasonal
- **Episode length**: ~30–45 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

General listeners who want myth-busting and evidence-based takes on trending health/science topics.

## Notable episodes

- Episodes on psychedelics, vaping, diets, psychedelics, and pandemic-era questions.
- "Pandemic" specials during COVID.

## Reach & assessment

- A flagship Gimlet/Spotify science show; well-regarded for accessibility and rigor.
- Business model: Spotify / ads.
