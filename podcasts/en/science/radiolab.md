# Radiolab

## Summary

A pioneering narrative documentary podcast exploring science, philosophy, and the unexplained through immersive sound design and storytelling. One of the shows that established "podcast as art form."

## Hosts

- **Lulu Miller** — Co-founder of Radiolab's later era; writer/reporter.
- **Latif Nasser** — Reporter/host; known for narrative reporting.
- Founder Jad Abumrad departed the show in 2022.

## Format

- **Category**: Science / ideas documentary
- **Format**: Narrative-reported, sound-design-rich
- **Cadence**: Seasonal
- **Episode length**: ~40–60 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Curious general listeners; fans of long-form audio storytelling and science-humanities crossover.

## Notable episodes

- "Colors," "Space," and other iconic themed episodes from the Jad Abumrad era.
- Latif Nasser's investigative reporting (e.g., the "Other Latif" series).

## Reach & assessment

- A landmark podcast; Peabody winner and major influence on narrative audio craft.
- Business model: WNYC/NPR sponsorship + member support.
