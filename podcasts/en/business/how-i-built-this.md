# How I Built This with Guy Raz

## Summary

NPR / Wondery's flagship narrative interview show, hosted by veteran NPR correspondent Guy Raz, walking through the founding stories of iconic companies with their founders. One of the most popular English-language business podcasts and a staple of the founder-narrative genre — accessible, story-driven, and heavily cited by operators, VCs, and general business listeners.

## Host

- **Guy Raz** — Longtime NPR international correspondent and host of NPR's chart-topping *TED Radio Hour*; one of the most recognizable voices in narrative business podcasting. Co-founded Wondery's kids' podcast studio Tinkercast (with Mindy Thomas and Meredith Halpern-Ranzer), producing *Wow in the World* (100M+ downloads) and the related "Wondery Kids & Family" slate. He also hosts *The Great Creators with Guy Raz* and *Wisdom From The Top* (NPR), and *The Rewind* for Spotify (2018).

## Format

- **Category**: Founder stories / business narrative
- **Format**: Long-form (60–80 min) host + founder interview, narrative-edited
- **Cadence**: Weekly (with bonus "Ask Guy Anything" and "How You Built That" minisodes)
- **Episode length**: ~60–80 minutes (regular); ~30–45 min (postscript "How You Built That")
- **Language**: English
- **Related shows**: *Wisdom From The Top*, *The Great Creators with Guy Raz*, *TED Radio Hour* (NPR), *Wow in the World* (Tinkercast / Wondery+)

## Audience

Founders, students, general business-curious listeners, and operators who want a "story-first" take on company-building. Highly accessible to non-experts — frequently recommended as a top-of-funnel entry into the business-narrative podcast space.

## Notable episodes

- **Spanx (Sara Blakely)** — bootstrapping a billion-dollar apparel brand.
- **Warby Parker (Neil Blumenthal & team)** — direct-to-consumer disruption of eyewear.
- **Casper (Philip Krim)** — the DTC mattress category build-and-bust cycle.
- **Airbnb (Brian Chesky)** — narrative re-told around the 2020 IPO era.
- **Bumble (Whitney Wolfe Herd)** — founder-story angle on a category-defining dating app.
- **Tate's Bake Shop (Kathleen King)** — the $500M second-act exit (rebuilt after the first brand's collapse).
- **Discovery Channel & Curiosity Stream (John Hendricks)** — long-arc media founder stories.
- **Stitch Fix (Katrina Lake)** — data-driven retail founder narrative.
- **"Ask Guy Anything"** minisodes — meta-episode Q&As with Guy Raz on craft and process.

## Reach & assessment

- One of the most popular business podcasts in English; mainstream reach on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and NPR One.
- Inspired a 2020 best-selling companion book (*How I Built This: The Unexpected Paths to Success from the World's Most Inspiring Entrepreneurs*, Houghton Mifflin Harcourt; WSJ #1 business book, NYT top 10) — with foreword-style praise from Adam Grant, Eric Ries, Angela Duckworth.
- Host is a perennial judge/panelist at the **Webby Awards** (e.g., 2017 judges alongside Jimmy Kimmel, Questlove, Vint Cerf).
- Production / distribution: NPR + Wondery (Amazon). Plus a **Wondery+ premium tier** (ad-free + early access).
- Live "How I Built This Live" events recorded in front of audiences.
- Business model: NPR sponsorship (messaging from sponsors like Rocket Money, SimpliSafe, LinkedIn Premium) + Wondery ad sales + Wondery+ subscriptions + book + live events.
