# How I Built This

## Summary

NPR / Wondery narrative interview show where host Guy Raz walks through the founding stories of iconic companies with their founders. Accessible, story-driven, and a staple of English business podcasts.

## Host

- **Guy Raz** — Former NPR host and correspondent; built a mini-empire of narrative business podcasts.

## Format

- **Category**: Founder stories / business narrative
- **Format**: Host + founder interview, narrative-edited
- **Cadence**: Weekly
- **Episode length**: ~60–80 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Broad: entrepreneurs, students, general business-curious listeners. Highly accessible to non-experts.

## Notable episodes

- Origin stories of household-name consumer brands and tech companies.
- "How I Built This Live" event recordings.

## Reach & assessment

- One of the most popular business podcasts in English; mainstream reach.
- Business model: NPR/Wondery sponsorship + live tours.
