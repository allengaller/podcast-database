# Masters of Scale with Reid Hoffman

## Summary

A theory-driven, narrative-produced business podcast hosted by Reid Hoffman — LinkedIn co-founder, Greylock partner, and one of Silicon Valley's most prominent thinker-investors. Each episode is built around a "surprising" thesis (e.g., *blitzscaling*, *network effects*, *company culture*, *fail forward*) and tested against high-profile founder interviews. Originally launched in partnership with LinkedIn in 2017, later produced by Hoffman's media company WaitWhat and distributed via a Spotify exclusive window.

## Host

- **Reid Hoffman** — Co-founder of LinkedIn (sold to Microsoft for $26.2B in 2016), partner at Greylock Partners, and co-author of *The Startup of You* / *The Alliance* / *Blitzscaling* (with Chris Yeh). He sits on the boards of Microsoft, Joby Aviation, Coda, and a number of early-stage AI companies.
- **Producers**: WaitWhat (founded by Hoffman and June Cohen, ex-TED head of media), with rotating executive producers across seasons.

## Format

- **Category**: Scaling theory / leadership / operator interviews
- **Format**: Host narrative (Hoffman sets the thesis) + guest founder interview + master-class style "open questions"
- **Cadence**: Seasonal — typically a flagship season per year, plus shorter topical mini-series ("Rapid Response" with Bob Safian, "Super Founders" with Ali Tamaseb, and *Masters of Scale: Summit* in-conversation specials)
- **Episode length**: ~35–50 minutes (main episodes); 15–25 minutes (Rapid Response)
- **Language**: English
- **Related shows**: *Masters of Scale: Rapid Response* (Bob Safian host), *Super Founders* (Ali Tamaseb host), *Masters of Scale Summit* live series, the *Blitzscaling* book & course (with Chris Yeh)

## Audience

Founders, executives, and operators interested in company-building theory, scaling playbooks, and a curated take on the canon of "Silicon Valley-grade" founder stories. Strong overlap with the Greylock / LinkedIn / Blitzscaling reading lists.

## Notable episodes

- **Howard Schultz (Starbucks)** — leadership and the "Third Place" thesis.
- **Mark Zuckerberg (Facebook)** — early blitzscaling lessons.
- **Sheryl Sandberg (Facebook / Lean In)** — scaling organizational culture.
- **Brian Chesky (Airbnb)** — founder stories on operating through crisis.
- **Eric Schmidt (Google)** — lessons from scaling technical organizations.
- **Anna Wintour (Vogue)** — scaling a creative institution.
- **Satya Nadella (Microsoft)** — culture-transformation playbook.
- **"How to build a company in an AI-first world"** — themed season on AI-era scaling (Hoffman is a frequent voice in AI policy / agentic systems discourse).
- **Rapid Response episodes** with Bob Safian — focused on real-time crisis / pivot / wartime leadership (recorded during COVID-19, 2020 election, 2023 AI inflection, etc.).

## Reach & assessment

- A flagship produced business podcast, well-regarded for production value (often cited alongside *How I Built This* and *a16z Podcast* as the "big three" of the founder-interview genre).
- 2019: WaitWhat signed an exclusive multi-year partnership with Spotify, briefly making the show a Spotify exclusive. The arrangement ended and the show is now broadly distributed.
- 2020+: Cross-licensed to **MasterClass** as *Masters of Scale* video lectures (Reid Hoffman course).
- Available in Chinese on Ximalaya (喜马拉雅) — early translated distribution into Mandarin.
- **Business model**: sponsorship (L'Oreal, Nike, WeWork in earlier seasons; Salesforce and others in newer seasons) + cross-promotion with Greylock portfolio + book tie-ins + MasterClass licensing + live events.
