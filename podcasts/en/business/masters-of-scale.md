# Masters of Scale

## Summary

Reid Hoffman (LinkedIn co-founder and Greylock investor) presents theories of how companies scale, each episode built around a "surprising" thesis and tested against interviews with iconic founders. Highly produced, narrative-driven.

## Host

- **Reid Hoffman** — Co-founder of LinkedIn, partner at Greylock; one of Silicon Valley's most prominent thinker-investors.

## Format

- **Category**: Scaling / leadership
- **Format**: Host narrative + guest founder interviews
- **Cadence**: Seasonal
- **Episode length**: ~35–50 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Founders, executives, operators interested in scaling theory and leadership.

## Notable episodes

- Thesis-driven episodes on network effects, blitzscaling, and culture.
- Interviews with unicorn founders framed around the episode's thesis.

## Reach & assessment

- A flagship produced business podcast; well-regarded for production value.
- Business model: sponsorship + originally LinkedIn partnership.
