# SaaStr Podcast

## Summary

The official podcast of SaaStr — the largest community and annual conference series for B2B / SaaS founders, CEOs, and go-to-market leaders. Hosted primarily by **Jason Lemkin** (founder & CEO of SaaStr), the show blends deep founder interviews, GTM "war stories," and short-form tactical episodes on hiring, scaling, sales, and (since 2023) the AI-first B2B rebuild. Closely tied to the **SaaStr Annual** conference in the San Francisco Bay Area (Sep 9–11, 2025; 10th edition in 2024), the **SaaStr Europa** event in London (now SaaStr AI London, Dec 1–2, 2025), and the SaaStr.ai content vertical.

## Host

- **Jason Lemkin** — Founder & CEO of SaaStr, Inc. (founded 2012). Former SaaS founder/operator (two-time CEO: Fanatics/Vistaprint alum; Echosign/SaaS-track); now an early-stage investor and the de facto godfather of the modern SaaS GTM canon. Author of the most-cited SaaS-founder essays on **CAC payback, NRR (net revenue retention), Rule of 40, the "70% first VP Sales fail" thesis**, and the "10 Common Mistakes Founders Make Hiring Their First VP Sales" talk (re-delivered annually at SaaStr Annual).
- Co-hosts and on-stage interviewers vary widely — for marquee Annual conversations, Lemkin often sits down with CEOs of Klaviyo (Andrew Bialecki), Notion, HubSpot, Zoom, Monday.com, etc.

## Format

- **Category**: SaaS / B2B / GTM / AI-first rebuilding of SaaS
- **Format**: Long-form host + guest interview (Annual marquee conversations are 30–60 min), plus short-form solo tactical episodes (10–20 min) on pricing, hiring, churn, sales comp, and AI product integration.
- **Cadence**: Weekly (rotating between short tactical episodes and longer deep cuts); the Annual event drives a yearly burst of "official conversation" episodes in Q3.
- **Episode length**: ~10–20 min (short tactical) / ~30–60 min (Annual / deep dive)
- **Language**: English
- **Related shows / ecosystem**:
  - **SaaStr Annual** — the SF Bay Area conference (Sep 9–11, 2025), described by Eric Yuan (Zoom CEO) as "the SuperBowl of B2B."
  - **SaaStr Europa** (now SaaStr AI London) — the European edition, 2,500+ attendees, 75+ sessions, 300+ VCs, 2,000+ meetings (Dec 1–2, 2025 at Park Plaza Westminster Bridge).
  - **SaaStr AI** — a dedicated content/vertical on the AI-first B2B rebuild.
  - YouTube is the primary long-form archive; the podcast feed is largely the audio version of those recordings.

## Audience

Founders, CEOs, CROs/heads-of-sales, product and growth leaders at B2B / SaaS companies, and the broader investor + operator community tracking GTM and AI-first product strategy. Particularly influential with early- and growth-stage SaaS teams in the **$1M–$100M ARR** range.

## Notable episodes / topic threads

- **"10 Common Mistakes Founders Make Hiring Their First VP Sales"** — Lemkin's signature talk (annually re-staged since 2014, updated with AI-era data). The 2024 update emphasises 70% first-VP-Sales fail rate, the "founder-mode" trap, and the necessity for the VP to be a product expert.
- **Klaviyo (Andrew Bialecki)** — the marquee Annual conversation: Klaviyo's PLG-then-enterprise journey, NDR of 117%, the path to 2023 IPO, and the $1B ARR run.
- **"AI Is Now a Base Feature of Every SaaS"** — Lemkin's 2024 SaaStr Annual keynote arguing that 2024 is the inflection where AI became table stakes, not a differentiator; covers "Sell work, not software" pricing, hybrid pricing (60%+ of AI companies use it from day one, with 3x NDR lift), and the move from seats to "value-based" / consumption pricing.
- **Point Nine (Christoph Janz)** on fundraising data — the canonical deck of cohort, CAC, magic-number, and Rule of 40 metrics.
- **Palantir "BLG / Bootcamp-led growth"** — recurring guest on the FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) model and AIP Bootcamp as enterprise GTM.
- **Kustomer** — the "cancel subscription, go agent-based" case study (one of the most-discussed pricing model flips of 2024).
- **Monday.com (Work OS + seat→seat-based + viewer pricing)** — the canonical "hyperfunctional" case study.

## Reach & assessment

- The podcast is a major distribution channel for the SaaStr Annual + SaaStr AI London content; the YouTube channel is widely cited by B2B operators (often the canonical source for Lemkin's annual "State of SaaS" / GTM Survey data).
- **Annual metrics (public)**:
  - SaaStr Annual 2024: ~10th anniversary edition (Sep 10–12, 2024, San Mateo County Events Center), with high C-level + international attendance.
  - SaaStr AI London 2025: 2,500+ attendees, 75+ sessions, 300+ VCs, 2,000+ curated meetings.
- The show is a **primary GTM-canon source**: Lemkin's essays and SaaStr Annual talks are cited in YC partner notes, a16z and Bessemer operator surveys, and a wide range of operator Slack communities.
- **Business model**: conference + content ecosystem. SaaStr earns primarily from conference tickets, sponsorships, and the SaaStr AI / content products; the podcast is a top-of-funnel / brand channel for those products.
- Notable industry signal: Eric Yuan (Zoom), Eran Zinman (Monday.com), Dharmesh Shah (HubSpot), and Ariel Cohen (Navan) have all publicly credited SaaStr as a major reference point for scaling strategy.
