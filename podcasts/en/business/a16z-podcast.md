# a16z Podcast

## Summary

The official podcast network of venture firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) — covering tech, startups, and the firm's investment theses across AI, crypto/web3, bio/healthcare, enterprise, fintech, and growth. Functions as a direct channel for one of Silicon Valley's most influential VC brands, and a primary venue for the firm to publish its POV, frameworks, and founder/tooling playbooks.

## Hosts

- **Sonal Chokshi** — Editor in Chief of a16z and host across most of the firm's podcast feeds; joined in 2018 from *FiveThirtyEight* / *The Atlantic*. Hosts the flagship "a16z Podcast" + "web3 with a16z crypto" + "16 Minutes" + "a16z Live".
- **Rotating general partners** — Marc Andreessen, Ben Horowitz, Andrew Chen (consumer growth), Jeff Jordan (marketplaces), Connie Chan (consumer + China), David George (growth), Vijay Pande (bio/health), Chris Dixon (crypto + a16z crypto), Angela Strange (fintech), and others contribute frequently.
- **Editorial**: a16z maintains a dedicated editorial / content team; the podcast is treated as a publication, not a side channel.

## Format

- **Category**: VC thesis / tech analysis / founder / growth frameworks
- **Format**: Conversational — host + 1–2 guests (a16z partners, founders, operators, academics)
- **Cadence**: Multiple per week (across the network of feeds)
- **Episode length**: ~30–60 minutes
- **Language**: English
- **The network of feeds (2024–2025)**:
  - **a16z Podcast** — the main general-interest feed (covers a16z's POV across the stack).
  - **16 Minutes** — short (16-min) news + thesis roundups, typically recorded weekly.
  - **a16z Live** — live recordings, conference content, and stage interviews.
  - **web3 with a16z crypto** (now "a16z crypto") — the dedicated crypto/web3 feed; covers infra, regulation, network effects, moats, etc. (e.g., the popular *Network Effects, Moats, & the Business of web3* episode).
  - **Bio + Health** (themed series under the a16z bio fund).
  - **Idea Maze (omnibus re-themes)** — narrative episodes.

## Audience

Founders, operators, investors, and policy watchers interested in a16z's worldview. The feeds are widely cited by YC founders, growth marketers (Andrew Chen's L28 / DAU-MAU / cohort-curvature essays and audio), and crypto builders (a16z crypto's regulatory and token-launch frameworks are referenced by US/EU policy teams).

## Notable episodes / topic threads

- **Andrew Chen × Jeff Jordan on growth** — the canonical "growth = acquisition + engagement + retention" framework (transcribed widely, including on andrewchen.com).
- **Chris Dixon × Scott Duke Kominers** — *Network Effects, Moats, & the Business of web3* — the deep-dive on network-effect theory in a post-source-available world.
- **a16z crypto airdrop framework** — multi-episode series on how to design token distributions that avoid Sybil attacks, align with long-term governance, and pass regulatory scrutiny (covered in *web3 with a16z crypto*).
- **Marc Andreessen's "Why Software Is Eating the World"** — the canonical essay and follow-on audio (originally 2011, recurring reference).
- **Bio/healthcare thesis** — Vijay Pande and others on AI-driven drug discovery, GLP-1 / obesity-drug markets, longevity, and the "biotech as software" thesis.
- **AI thesis** — recurring coverage of agentic systems, the "GPT-x app layer," the "Why vertical AI has higher moats than horizontal AI" framework, and the firm's substantial investments in OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Character.AI, etc.
- **Enterprise / GTM** — conversations with the operating partners on cloud marketplaces, sales-led vs. product-led, and the "Build for the Buyer" series.
- **Idea Maze** — recurring essays on "what could the future look like in a particular area, and which path is most likely to win."

## Reach & assessment

- Among the most established VC-branded podcast networks in English; a primary distribution surface for a16z's POV to founders, operators, and policy makers.
- The crypto feed (web3 with a16z crypto / a16z crypto) is the most-cited crypto industry podcast for regulatory + token-economics discussions, regularly referenced in US Congressional and SEC comment letters (the firm publishes a public regulatory framework and a list of policy interventions).
- The growth episodes (Andrew Chen × Jeff Jordan) are widely cited in operator Slack groups and growth newsletters; the conversations on DAU/MAU, L28, cohort curves, network-effect flywheels, and the "smile" engagement curve are referenced in many MBA and growth-program syllabi.
- **Business model**: owned and operated by the firm; functions as a brand/marketing surface for a16z's funds and portfolio (a16z is an investment adviser — not broker-dealer — and the podcast publishing sits under the firm's "ideas/content" function per a16z.com/disclosures).
- Distribution is across major podcast platforms; full transcripts are typically posted on a16z.com and a16zcrypto.com.
