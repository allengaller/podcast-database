# a16z Podcast

## Summary

The official podcast of venture firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), covering tech, startups, and the firm's investment theses across areas like AI, crypto, bio, and enterprise. A direct channel for one of tech's most influential VC brands.

## Hosts

- **Rotating** — Hosted by various a16z general partners and editorial staff; format has evolved over the years (e.g., the "a16z Podcast" vs. "web3 with a16z crypto" feeds).

## Format

- **Category**: VC / tech analysis
- **Format**: Conversations among partners + founder/expert guests
- **Cadence**: Multiple per week (across feeds)
- **Episode length**: ~30–60 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Founders, operators, investors, and policy watchers interested in a16z's worldview.

## Notable episodes

- AI thesis and market-structure discussions.
- Crypto/web3 and bio policy episodes.

## Reach & assessment

- Among the most established VC-branded podcasts; influential for distributing a16z's POV.
- Business model: firm-owned, brand/marketing function.
