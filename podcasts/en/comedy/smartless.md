# SmartLess

## Summary

Comedy interview podcast where one of the three actor-hosts reveals a mystery celebrity guest to the other two. Casual, funny Hollywood conversations built on the hosts' real friendships.

## Hosts

- **Jason Bateman** — Actor/director.
- **Sean Hayes** — Actor (*Will & Grace*).
- **Will Arnett** — Actor/comedian (*Arrested Development*).

## Format

- **Category**: Comedy / celebrity interview
- **Format**: Three hosts + one guest
- **Cadence**: Weekly
- **Episode length**: ~60–90 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Pop-culture and comedy fans; broad mainstream audience drawn by A-list guests.

## Notable episodes

- Interviews with major actors, musicians, and public figures.
- Live tour recordings.

## Reach & assessment

- A top-tier comedy podcast; notable Amazon/Wondery exclusive deal.
- Business model: Wondery/Amazon deal + sponsorships + live tours.
