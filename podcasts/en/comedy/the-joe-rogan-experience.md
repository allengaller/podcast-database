# The Joe Rogan Experience

## Summary

One of the most-listened podcasts in the world: long, free-form conversations between comedian Joe Rogan and guests spanning comedy, MMA, science, politics, business, and culture. A cultural force with enormous reach and frequent controversy.

## Host

- **Joe Rogan** — Stand-up comedian, UFC commentator, and TV host; turned the show into arguably the biggest podcast in English.

## Format

- **Category**: Long-form interview / comedy
- **Format**: Host + single guest, free-flowing
- **Cadence**: Multiple per week
- **Episode length**: Often 2–4+ hours
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Mass, broad audience — far beyond typical podcast listeners; skews male and is highly influential on cultural and political discourse.

## Notable episodes

- High-profile interviews with scientists, politicians, entrepreneurs, and celebrities.
- Recurring "protect your neck" / fight-companion episodes with comedians.

## Reach & assessment

- Consistently among the #1 podcasts globally; a major driver of Spotify's podcast strategy.
- Business model: exclusive Spotify deal + sponsorships.
- Note: frequently controversial; episodes have sparked misinformation / platform-policy debates.

## 2026 update (Forbes 最高收入榜)

- **Forbes 2026 收入榜首**:年入 **$82M** (#1),统计周期 2025-06 ~ 2026-06,稳居全球收入最高播客主持人。
- **2024 Spotify 续约**:新签 3 年合约,总价值最高达 **$250M**(以最低保底 + 分成模式)。
- **量级**:每月更新约 15 期,下载/播放量合计超 **8000 万次**。
- **2024 美国大选**:公开表态支持 Donald Trump,引发广泛讨论,也让播客行业整体影响力被重新评估。
