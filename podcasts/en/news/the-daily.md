# The Daily

## Summary

The New York Times' flagship daily news podcast: one major story each weekday, told with high production value by Times journalists. A defining show that reshaped how news is consumed in audio.

## Hosts

- **Michael Barbaro** — Longtime NYT political reporter; original and most recognized host.
- **Sabrina Tavernise** — NYT reporter; joined as co-host in the show's later rotation.

## Format

- **Category**: Daily news
- **Format**: Host + NYT reporters, narrative-edited
- **Cadence**: Weekdays
- **Episode length**: ~20–30 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Mass audience; the most-listened news podcast in many English markets. Reaches far beyond typical podcast listeners.

## Notable episodes

- Major political and global events (elections, conflicts, Supreme Court).
- Long-running series tied to NYT investigations.

## Reach & assessment

- Among the most-downloaded podcasts in the world; set the template for daily news podcasts.
- Business model: NYT sponsorship / subscription.
