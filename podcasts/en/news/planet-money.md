# Planet Money

## Summary

NPR's economics podcast that explains the economy through stories — making abstract economic concepts vivid and accessible. A long-running benchmark for explanatory business/econ journalism.

## Hosts

- **Rotating** — NPR economics reporters, including Kenny Malone, Amanda Aronczyk, Erika Beras, Keith Romer, Wailin Wong, and others.

## Format

- **Category**: Economics / business explainer
- **Format**: Narrative-reported, multi-host
- **Cadence**: Weekly (plus "Indicator" daily spin-off)
- **Episode length**: ~20–30 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

General listeners curious about how the economy and business work; highly accessible.

## Notable episodes

- The long-running "t-shirt" project (tracing a t-shirt from cotton to consumer).
- Explainers on financial crises, tariffs, and labor markets.

## Reach & assessment

- A flagship NPR podcast; influential in defining narrative econ journalism.
- Business model: NPR sponsorship / member support.
