# Today in Focus

## Summary

The Guardian's daily news podcast, taking one in-depth story per episode from the Guardian's global newsroom. A leading UK-origin daily news show with strong international coverage.

## Hosts

- **Nosheen Iqbal** — Lead host; supported by rotating Guardian reporters and correspondents.

## Format

- **Category**: Daily news
- **Format**: Host + Guardian correspondent, narrative-edited
- **Cadence**: Weekdays
- **Episode length**: ~20–30 minutes
- **Language**: English (UK)

## Audience

News-focused listeners, especially UK and internationally-minded audiences wanting non-US-centric coverage.

## Notable episodes

- Deep coverage of UK politics, global conflicts, and long-form investigations.
- Big-news-day explainers.

## Reach & assessment

- One of the leading UK daily news podcasts; high journalistic credibility.
- Business model: Guardian sponsorship / reader contributions.
