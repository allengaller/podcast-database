# Acquired

## Summary

A long-form podcast that tells the story of how great companies are built, through deeply researched multi-hour episodes on acquisitions, IPOs, and company histories. Arguably the defining "business history" show of the English-language podcast space.

## Hosts

- **Ben Gilbert** — Managing director of Madrona Venture Labs; deep on venture and tech strategy.
- **David Rosenthal** — Former product and investing leader; brings operating and strategy perspective.

## Format

- **Category**: Business history / tech strategy
- **Format**: Two-host research-intensive narrative; occasional interviews
- **Cadence**: Biweekly-ish (plus live episodes and "Apply" spin-offs)
- **Episode length**: Typically 2–4 hours
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Founders, investors, product/strategy leaders, and serious business nerds who want exhaustive, well-sourced company deep dives rather than hot takes.

## Notable episodes

- Mega-episodes on companies like NVIDIA, Berkshire Hathaway, TSMC, and the "season finale" style franchise histories.
- Live event recordings covering iconic acquisitions (e.g., the famous "the story of TSMC").

## Reach & assessment

- Widely regarded as one of the best business podcasts in English; a benchmark for long-form research quality.
- Business model: sponsorships from major tech/finance brands; live events.
