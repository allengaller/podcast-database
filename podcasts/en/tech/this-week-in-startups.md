# This Week in Startups

## Summary

Long-running English podcast on startups, venture capital, and founder journeys, hosted by prominent angel investor Jason Calacanis. Mixes founder interviews, VC news, and tactical founder advice.

## Host

- **Jason Calacanis** — Angel investor (early Uber, etc.), founder of the LAUNCH fund and This Week in Startups; also an All-In host.

## Format

- **Category**: Startups / VC
- **Format**: Host + guest interviews, plus news segments
- **Cadence**: Multiple per week
- **Episode length**: ~45–90 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Founders, aspiring entrepreneurs, angel investors, and startup-curious listeners.

## Notable episodes

- Founder-origin-story interviews with notable unicorn founders.
- Tactical episodes on fundraising, hiring, and go-to-market.

## Reach & assessment

- One of the longest-running startup podcasts; consistently high output volume.
- Business model: sponsorships + events (LAUNCH festival).
