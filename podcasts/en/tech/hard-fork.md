# Hard Fork

## Summary

A weekly tech news podcast from The New York Times, hosted by two veteran tech journalists, covering the week's biggest tech and AI stories with informed, accessible banter. Quickly became a flagship tech-news show after launch.

## Hosts

- **Kevin Roose** — Technology columnist at The New York Times.
- **Casey Newton** — Founding editor of Platformer; veteran tech and platforms journalist.

## Format

- **Category**: Tech news / analysis
- **Format**: Two-host conversation + segments + interviews
- **Cadence**: Weekly
- **Episode length**: ~60–75 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

General tech-interested listeners, journalists, founders, and anyone wanting a reliable weekly digest of AI/tech news from mainstream-credible voices.

## Notable episodes

- Weekly AI news roundups during the generative-AI boom.
- Deep-dives on platform policy, social media, and tech culture.

## Reach & assessment
