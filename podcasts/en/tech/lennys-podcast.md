# Lenny's Podcast

## Summary

Interviews with world-class product leaders, founders, and operators on how great products are built and how careers in tech are navigated. Grew out of the Lenny's Newsletter Substack into one of the most influential product-management podcasts in English.

## Host

- **Lenny Rachitsky** — Former Airbnb product lead, now an independent writer/investor; one of the most followed PM voices globally via his newsletter and podcast.

## Format

- **Category**: Product / startups / career
- **Format**: Long-form guest interviews, structured around actionable frameworks
- **Cadence**: Weekly
- **Episode length**: ~60–120 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Product managers, founders, designers, and tech operators seeking tactical, high-signal career and building advice.

## Notable episodes

- Interviews with CPOs/CEOs of major tech companies on org design and product craft.
- "How to break into product" and career-strategy episodes.

## Reach & assessment

- Top-tier business/tech podcast by reach; routinely in Apple Podcasts business charts.
- Business model: sponsorships + paid newsletter/community.
