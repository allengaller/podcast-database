# Lex Fridman Podcast

## Summary

Extremely long-form interview podcast spanning AI, science, technology, philosophy, and culture, hosted by MIT-affiliated researcher Lex Fridman. Known for multi-hour, slow-paced, deeply exploratory conversations with high-profile guests.

## Host

- **Lex Fridman** — Research scientist (originally in autonomous vehicles / AI at MIT); became one of the most-listened podcasters in English.

## Format

- **Category**: Long-form interview / AI & ideas
- **Format**: Single guest, long-form conversation
- **Cadence**: Weekly+
- **Episode length**: Often 2–4+ hours
- **Language**: English

## Audience

AI researchers, engineers, students, and curious general listeners; large crossover audience due to famous guests.

## Notable episodes

- Interviews with leading AI figures (researchers and CEOs of major AI labs).
- Conversations with scientists, entrepreneurs, and public intellectuals.

## Reach & assessment

- One of the most-listened long-form interview podcasts globally; enormous YouTube presence.
- Business model: sponsorships (notably high-volume reads) + direct support.
