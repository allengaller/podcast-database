# All-In Podcast

## Summary

Four prominent tech investors debate tech, markets, macro, and politics each week. Combines insider VC/tech perspective with opinionated (often contrarian) political commentary, which has made it both hugely popular and frequently controversial.

## Hosts

- **Chamath Palihapitiya** — VC, former Facebook executive.
- **Jason Calacanis** — Angel investor, founder of This Week in Startups.
- **David Sacks** — Entrepreneur/investor (Craft Ventures); later entered U.S. government roles.
- **David Friedberg** — Science/tech entrepreneur; brings a research-driven, science-lens.

## Format

- **Category**: VC / tech & political commentary
- **Format**: Four-host roundtable discussion
- **Cadence**: Weekly
- **Episode length**: ~90–120 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

VCs, founders, finance professionals, and politically-engaged tech followers; large general audience due to the crossover into politics.

## Notable episodes

- Weekly news roundups ("kings of crypto/tech/VC" takes on markets and policy).
- Election-cycle and macro-themed specials.

## Reach & assessment

- One of the most-discussed English tech podcasts; a key vector of "VC consensus/maverick" takes.
- Business model: sponsorships + live "All-In Summit" events.
