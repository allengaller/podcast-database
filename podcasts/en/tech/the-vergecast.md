# The Vergecast

## Summary

The flagship podcast of The Verge, covering the week in consumer tech, platforms, and tech policy. A reliable, journalistically grounded weekly digest of the tech industry's biggest stories.

## Host

- **Nilay Patel** — Editor-in-chief of The Verge; brings a legal/policy-aware lens to tech news.

## Format

- **Category**: Tech news / consumer tech
- **Format**: Host + Verge reporters/guests; interview segments
- **Cadence**: Weekly (plus extras)
- **Episode length**: ~60–90 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

General tech consumers, enthusiasts, and industry watchers wanting mainstream-credible tech news.

## Notable episodes

- Weekly news roundups with Verge reporters.
- Major exec interviews (CEOs of platform companies).

## Reach & assessment

- A long-running, top-tier tech-news podcast; among the most established in English.
- Business model: Vox Media sponsorships.
