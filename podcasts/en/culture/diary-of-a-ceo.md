# The Diary Of A CEO

## Summary

Long-form interview podcast by entrepreneur Steven Bartlett, blending founder stories, behavioral science, health, and personal-development conversations with high-profile guests. Exploded in reach via YouTube, becoming one of the most-watched interview shows globally.

## Host

- **Steven Bartlett** — Founder of Social Chain, investor, Dragon on *Dragons' Den*; author.

## Format

- **Category**: Long-form interview
- **Format**: Host + single guest, narrative intro
- **Cadence**: Weekly+
- **Episode length**: ~60–120 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Young professionals, entrepreneurs, and general audiences interested in psychology, success, and health; very large YouTube-driven audience.

## Notable episodes

- Interviews with scientists, founders, athletes, and public figures on behavior, health, and success.
- Founder-origin episodes.

## Reach & assessment

- Among the fastest-growing interview podcasts of the 2020s; massive YouTube presence.
- Business model: sponsorships + book/business ecosystem.
