# Fresh Air

## Summary

NPR's legendary interview show covering arts, culture, and current affairs. Anchored for decades by Terry Gross — one of the most celebrated interviewers in American media — it remains a benchmark for the craft of the interview.

## Hosts

- **Terry Gross** — Host since 1975; renowned for preparation and the quality of her questions.
- **Tonya Mosley** — Joined as co-host in the 2020s as part of succession planning.

## Format

- **Category**: Interview / arts & ideas
- **Format**: Host + guest interviews, pre-produced
- **Cadence**: Weekdays
- **Episode length**: ~40–45 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Long-standing NPR audience: arts, culture, and journalism lovers; highly educated core.

## Notable episodes

- Iconic author, filmmaker, musician, and newsmaker interviews.
- Memorable and sometimes famously tense political interviews.

## Reach & assessment

- One of the longest-running and most-respected interview shows in American broadcasting; Peabody winner.
- Business model: NPR / WHYY sponsorship + member support.
