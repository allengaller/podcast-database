# The Tim Ferriss Show

## Summary

Long-form interview show where Tim Ferriss deconstructs the routines, tactics, and mindsets of world-class performers across business, sports, science, and the arts. A foundational show that helped define the modern "interview the elite" genre.

## Host

- **Tim Ferriss** — Author of *The 4-Hour Workweek*; investor and self-experimenter; one of the earliest breakout podcasters.

## Format

- **Category**: Long-form interview / performance deconstruction
- **Format**: Host + single guest, deep Q&A
- **Cadence**: Weekly
- **Episode length**: Often 2–3+ hours
- **Language**: English

## Audience

Entrepreneurs, self-improvers, athletes, creatives; broad general audience.

## Notable episodes

- "Tools of Titans"-style interviews with investors, athletes, scientists, and artists.
- Tactical episodes on routines, habits, and decision-making.

## Reach & assessment

- One of the most-downloaded interview podcasts of all time; hugely influential.
- Business model: sponsorships + book/community ecosystem.
