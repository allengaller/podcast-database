# Data Skeptic

## 简介

**Data Skeptic** is one of the longest-running English-language data science and AI podcasts, founded and hosted by **Kyle Polich** in 2014. The show is structured around a unique two-format model: **short "mini episodes" (10–15 minutes)** that explain a single data science / machine learning concept in an accessible, often interview-style format, and **longer "full episodes" (30–60 minutes)** that feature in-depth interviews with practicing data scientists, AI researchers, and engineers. Together, the two formats form a complementary "concept first, then conversation" learning arc.

The show's title is a self-aware nod to the data science community's tendency to be "skeptical" of claims without empirical evidence. The show's name, format, and editorial stance together make *Data Skeptic* a go-to "first listen" for newcomers to data science / AI, while still being substantive enough for practitioners. As of 2026, the show has produced close to 600 episodes, making it one of the most prolific shows in the space.

The show is hosted on `dataskeptic.com`, with audio mirrored to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, and the major podcast platforms via RSS.

## Hosts Background

- **Kyle Polich**:founder and executive producer of *Data Skeptic*. Background in data science and software engineering, with prior experience building data products. He is known for his clear, didactic interview style and his willingness to dive deep into the math when needed. Kyle also offers 1:1 career coaching for data scientists (via `calendly.com/polich`), and runs a number of community projects around the show.
- **Linh Da Pham**:Kyle's wife and the show's co-host, particularly on the mini episodes. Linh Da plays the "non-expert interviewer" role, asking the clarifying questions that a beginner might ask — a format that has become a signature of the show. Her questions often pull the most illuminating explanations out of the technical guests.

The husband-and-wife duo brings a unique dynamic to the show: Kyle represents the "expert who can explain," and Linh Da represents the "intelligent non-expert who knows what to ask." This makes the show unusually accessible.

## Content Style and Format

- **Two complementary formats**:
  1. **Mini episodes (10–15 minutes)**:short, concept-driven episodes that explain a single data science / AI topic (e.g., "graphs and networks," "latent Dirichlet allocation," "transformer architecture," "causal inference"). These often feature Linh Da asking Kyle to explain a concept, and serve as a "primer" for the longer interview.
  2. **Full episodes (30–60 minutes)**:in-depth interviews with practicing data scientists, AI researchers, and engineers, covering their work, papers, products, and career paths. Recent topics include LLMs, reinforcement learning, AI safety, statistical methods, and AI in industry.
- **Frequency**:biweekly, with a steady cadence of two episodes per week (one mini, one full).
- **Production style**:simple, conversational, no-frills. The show prioritizes clarity and pedagogy over flashy production.
- **Editorial stance**:the show is "skeptical but not cynical." It engages with cutting-edge research, but always asks for evidence and intuition, not just hype.

## Audience Profile

- **Aspiring data scientists and ML engineers** — the show's mini episodes are a popular "first listen" for people entering the field.
- **Practicing data scientists** — the long-form interviews cover the work of senior practitioners and academic researchers, often with an "under the hood" feel.
- **AI researchers** — the show has a track record of hosting less-prominent but technically substantive guests (e.g., graduate students, postdocs, junior researchers at top labs), which has earned it a reputation as a place to "discover" emerging voices in the field.
- **General technically curious listeners** — the mini-episode format is approachable without requiring a deep technical background.

## Representative Episodes

The show has produced close to 600 episodes across its two formats. Representative topics include:

- **"Graphs and Networks" (mini)**:an accessible primer on graph theory and its applications in ML.
- **Transformer architecture deep dives**:the show has done multiple episodes breaking down the transformer, attention, and the math behind modern LLMs.
- **Reinforcement learning series**:interviews with RL researchers and practitioners, covering policy gradients, Q-learning, and RLHF.
- **Causal inference series**:episodes on Pearl's causal hierarchy, instrumental variables, and the limits of observational data.
- **LLM interpretability and safety**:interviews with researchers working on mechanistic interpretability, alignment, and AI safety.
- **Statistics and methodology**:episodes on Bayesian methods, frequentist vs. Bayesian, A/B testing, and the replication crisis.
- **AI in industry**:interviews with data scientists and ML engineers at companies like Google, Meta, Netflix, Spotify, Uber, and Stripe.
