# Hard Fork

## 简介

**Hard Fork** is a weekly tech-and-AI podcast from *The New York Times*, hosted by **Kevin Roose** (NYT tech columnist, author of *Futureproof* and *Young Money*) and **Casey Newton** (founder of *Platformer*, former *Verge* editor, author of *What's Next*). The show launched in 2023 as a successor to the duo's earlier *Rabbit Hole* podcast and the *NYT*'s *The Daily*-style tech coverage.

The show is structured around a mix of **breaking news analysis**, **deep dives into a single tech / AI topic**, and **occasional long interviews** with top tech operators, AI researchers, and regulators. In 2024–2026, Hard Fork has become a go-to podcast for understanding how AI is reshaping the tech industry, with regular coverage of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, the AI safety debate, and the intersection of AI with antitrust, labor, and elections.

Kevin and Casey have built a distinctive style: **one tech-optimist + one tech-skeptic** co-host dynamic, where Kevin tends to lean into the human / opportunity angle while Casey leans into the social / labor / regulatory angle. This makes the show especially well-suited for listeners who want AI coverage that takes both the upside and the downside seriously.

## Hosts Background

- **Kevin Roose**:NYT tech columnist since 2017, writes the widely-read "The Shift" newsletter and the "Hard Fork" column. Author of *Futureproof* (2021) and *Young Money* (2014). Known for his on-the-ground reporting from AI labs, his early 2023 conversation with Bing's Sydney chatbot, and his book-length exploration of AI companionship.
- **Casey Newton**:Founder of *Platformer*, a must-read newsletter on tech and democracy. Former senior editor at *The Verge*, where he led the tech-tip vertical. Author of *What's Next: Essays on Tech, Labor, and Inequality* (2024). Known for his weekly column at *Platformer* and his deep reporting on platform power, content moderation, and the future of work.

The pair previously co-hosted the *Rabbit Hole* podcast (NYT, 2020–2022), which focused on the social impact of technology.

## Content Style and Format

- **Weekly release**, typically Friday morning (NYT schedule). Episodes run 45–90 minutes.
- **Three-act structure**:
  1. **News roundup** — 2–3 of the week's biggest tech / AI stories, with each host taking a position.
  2. **Main segment / deep dive** — a single topic, often with a guest or pre-recorded interview. Recent deep-dive topics include Google's antitrust trial, the OpenAI / Microsoft restructure, the AI talent wars, and the 2024 / 2025 election + AI.
  3. **Listener mail / "Good News" or "Bad News" closer** — Kevin and Casey answer questions from the audience, often with humor.
- **Tone**:conversational, witty, but rigorous. Both hosts do real reporting and are unafraid to break news (e.g., the show has hosted first-look interviews with Sam Altman, Brad Smith, Lina Khan, and others).
- **Video**:the show is filmed in front of a small studio audience at NYT headquarters; full video is on YouTube; audio on Apple Podcasts / Spotify / RSS.

## Audience Profile

- **NYT readers and tech-industry professionals** — primary audience, skewing toward US-based tech operators, investors, and policy people.
- **AI builders and product managers** — use the show to track the week in AI strategy and policy.
- **Tech-curious general listeners** — the show's mix of humor and rigor makes it accessible to non-engineers.
- **Journalists and policy researchers** — Kevin and Casey are themselves sources, so the show is a primary venue for tech-policy news.
