# Eye on AI

## 简介

**Eye on AI** is a long-form English-language AI podcast hosted by **Jack Lindsay**, a former *New York Times* journalist. The show focuses on deep, hour-long interviews with the most important researchers, founders, and policy makers shaping the modern AI era. Since its launch in the late 2010s, the show has been a steady presence in the English-language AI podcast landscape, complementing shows like *Lex Fridman Podcast*, *The AI Podcast by NVIDIA*, and *Hard Fork* with a journalist's interview cadence and a research-first sensibility.

The show is structured as a single weekly interview (sometimes two), with each episode running 60–90 minutes. Jack's journalism background (NYT) shows in his preparation: he reads the guest's papers, products, and prior public statements, and asks pointed questions about both the technical and the social / political dimensions of AI. The show's name — "Eye on AI" — reflects this dual lens: both the technical "eye" of looking under the hood of AI systems, and the journalistic "eye" on the people, money, and policy behind them.

## Host Background

- **Jack Lindsay**:former *New York Times* journalist, with prior experience covering technology, finance, and global markets. After leaving the NYT, Jack launched *Eye on AI* to focus exclusively on the AI era. He is known for his calm, methodical interview style and for being unusually well-prepared for each guest.

Jack is widely respected in the AI research and operator community as a serious, non-hype host. The show is frequently cited in the AI research community, and several guests have noted that the interview was the most substantive they had done on a podcast.

## Content Style and Format

- **Weekly** single-interview episodes, typically 60–90 minutes.
- **Format**:one guest per episode, structured around three or four major themes: the guest's personal background, their current work, the technical / scientific details, and the broader implications for society, business, and policy.
- **Tone**:calm, journalistic, non-sensational. Jack tends to ask the same question a few different ways to get to the bottom of an issue, rather than playing "gotcha."
- **Distribution**:the show is available on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and via RSS; video is posted to YouTube. The show has a steady presence on the Apple Podcasts technology charts; specific subscriber / download numbers fluctuate, leave TODO.
- **Style distinction**:unlike *Lex Fridman* (philosophical, long-form, meandering) and *Hard Fork* (current events, witty, co-hosted), *Eye on AI* sits in the "serious one-on-one interview" lane alongside shows like *The Gradient Podcast*, *The Robot Brains Podcast*, and *Machine Learning Street Talk*.

## Audience Profile

- **AI researchers and engineers** — primary audience, who treat major *Eye on AI* episodes as a "must-listen" deep dive.
- **AI founders and operators** — particularly those who want a substantive interview without the hype.
- **AI policy researchers and journalists** — Jack's NYT background makes the show a natural fit for policy / governance audiences.
- **General intellectually curious listeners** — the show's hour-long interview format is approachable without requiring a deep technical background.

## Representative Episodes

The show has hosted a wide range of guests across the AI era, including:

- **Geoffrey Hinton (multiple, 2019–2024)**:the "Godfather of AI" interviews on the risks and opportunities of advanced AI.
- **Yann LeCun (multiple)**:Meta's chief AI scientist, the limits of LLMs, world models, and open-source AI.
- **Demis Hassabis (multiple)**:DeepMind's history, AlphaFold, the Nobel Prize for protein structure prediction, and the path to AGI.
- **Sam Altman (multiple)**:OpenAI's strategy, the GPT-5 / o-series roadmap, governance, and the Microsoft relationship.
- **Dario Amodei (multiple)**:Anthropic's strategy, Constitutional AI, the AGI race, and the politics of safe deployment.
- **Ilya Sutskever (post-OpenAI)**:leaving OpenAI, founding Safe Superintelligence Inc. (SSI), and the path to safe superintelligence.
- **Mira Murati (post-OpenAI / Thinking Machines)**:founding an independent AI lab, the future of open-source AI, and safety.
- **Fei-Fei Li (multiple)**:spatial intelligence, the "ImageNet moment," Stanford's HAI, and the policy dimensions of AI.
- **Andrew Ng (multiple)**:deep learning's history, the rise of AI agents, AI for everyone, and the future of education.
- **Yoshua Bengio (multiple)**:the Montreal AI ecosystem, the safety / alignment debate, and the future of academic AI research.
