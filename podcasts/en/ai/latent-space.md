# Latent Space

## Summary

"The AI Engineer Podcast" — by and for AI engineers. News, papers, and interviews focused on the models, tools, and ideas that practitioners use at work the next day. One of the most influential AI-engineering-native shows, paired with a widely-read newsletter/discord community.

## Hosts

- **swyx (Shawn Wang)** — AI engineer turned writer/community builder; runs Latent Space.
- **Alessio Fanelli** — Investor (Decibel Partners) and engineer; brings the VC + technical lens.

## Format

- **Category**: AI / AI engineering
- **Format**: Host + guest interviews (researchers, founders, infra builders)
- **Cadence**: Weekly+
- **Episode length**: ~60–90 minutes
- **Language**: English

## Audience

AI engineers, ML practitioners, infra/DevTools founders, and technical investors wanting grounded, practitioner-level signal on the model/tooling ecosystem.

## Notable episodes

- Deep-dives with model lab researchers and open-source infra builders.
- Demos/discussions of new agent frameworks, evals, and inference stacks.

## Reach & assessment

- Considered the reference podcast for the AI-engineering subfield; large newsletter + Discord amplifies reach.
- Business model: sponsorships + paid community/newsletter.
