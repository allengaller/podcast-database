# In Machines We Trust

## 简介

**In Machines We Trust** is an award-winning English-language AI podcast from **MIT Technology Review**, hosted by **Jennifer Strong**. The show "thoughtfully examines the far-reaching impact of artificial intelligence on our daily lives," exploring the rise of AI through the voices of people "reckoning with the power of the technology," and taking listeners "up close with the inventors and founders whose ambitions are fueling the development of new forms of AI, with far-reaching implications we're only just beginning to understand."

The show launched its first season in **August 2020**, with season two premiering on **March 31, 2021**, and has continued into multiple subsequent seasons. The show has appeared on podcast charts in **70+ countries**, reached **#1 on Apple's Tech charts**, and gained a spot in **Apple's Top 100 rankings across all categories**. The show's miniseries on the use of facial recognition technology by police won the **2020 Front Page Award** for best investigative reporting in audio, and is a **finalist for best limited series by the Publisher Podcast Awards**. The show's first season was shortlisted for **best podcast launch of the year** and **best tech show** at the Publisher Podcast Awards, and the show was nominated for an **Ambie** (the podcast industry's award for excellence in audio) as a finalist for **best knowledge, science, or tech podcast**.

Apple Podcasts named the series to its **New and Noteworthy** list upon launch, and the show also made **Best Podcasts of 2020** by Discover Pods and the **Top 100 of 2020** by Pocket Casts.

## Host & Production Background

- **Jennifer Strong(主持人)**:Senior editor and podcast host at MIT Technology Review, leading the publication's audio expansion. Strong has built a reputation as one of the most respected audio journalists covering AI, with a focus on the "lived experience" of AI deployment and the voices of people "reckoning with the power of the technology."
- **Anthony Green(制片人)**:Senior producer on the show, leading the editorial / production team behind the show's investigative and narrative structure.
- **Emma Cillekens(制片人)**:Producer on the show, focused on field reporting and guest coordination.

The show is published under the **MIT Technology Review** brand. Founded at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in **1899**, MIT Technology Review is a world-renowned, independent media company whose insight, analysis, reviews, interviews, and live events explain the newest technologies and their commercial, social, and political impact. The publication's authority derives from its relationship to the world's foremost technology institution and from its editors' deep technical knowledge, capacity to see technologies in their broadest context, and unequaled access to leading innovators and researchers.

## Content Style and Format

- **Format**:多季(season-based)+ 多集 miniseries(4-part miniseries)结构,典型单集 30–45 分钟;
- **Style**:journalistic, narrative-driven, investigative,与单纯 AI 公司创始人访谈类节目有显著区别——节目强调"AI 如何重塑普通人的日常生活",而非"AI 公司战略",代表"记者视角"而非"创业者视角";
- **典型内容**(从已公开的 S2 / S3 资料可见):
  - **4-part miniseries on how AI is used in hiring**(S2):Companies use algorithms to recognize people's emotions, analyze information from social media profiles, and factor in the results of psychological game play to determine whether a candidate is a good fit for a job;
  - **Beauty filters and the algorithm ranking of appearance**:how algorithms rank the way people look, and the results are influencing the things we do, the posts we see, and the way we think;
  - **Automation and our pocketbooks**:the shifting role of algorithms on credit scores, lending, trading, and pricing;
  - **Facial recognition and police surveillance**:the miniseries on the use of facial recognition technology by police;
  - **AI in housing and social services**:how facial recognition is quietly being used to control access to housing and social services;
  - **Digital humans and AI-generated likenesses**:how AI is helping birth digital humans that look and sound just like us;
  - **GPT / generative AI 浪潮的深度调查**:In Machines We Trust 进入第三季 / 第四季后,持续跟踪生成式 AI 的产业落地与社会影响。

## Audience Profile

- **MIT Technology Review 读者**:以"硬核技术 + 社会 / 政策视角"为诉求的高知读者,典型听众画像是技术决策者、学者、政策研究者、企业战略家;
- **AI 政策 / 监管 / 伦理研究者**:节目内容多聚焦"AI 治理 / 偏见 / 隐私 / 监控 / 招聘歧视"等议题,适合做政策研究;
- **新闻工作者 / 调查记者**:Front Page Award 加持,代表"播客作为调查性报道"形式的代表作品;
- **AI 产品 / 战略 / 投资从业者**:从节目获取"AI 社会影响"维度的判断,补充纯技术 / 商业视角的盲区;
- **公众与高等教育听众**:节目形式扎实、内容深度高,适合非工程师背景的"严肃听众"。

- **4-part miniseries on how AI is used in hiring**:Companies use algorithms to recognize people's emotions, analyze information from social media profiles, and factor in the results of psychological game play to determine whether a candidate is a good fit for a job — 节目对 AI 招聘歧视 / 情感识别偏见 / 心理测评操控的深度调查;
