# Dwarkesh Podcast

## Summary

Long-form interview show by Dwarkesh Patel, focused on leading AI researchers, scientists, and founders. Beloved in the AI-engineering/research community for deep, technically literate, multi-hour conversations with people building frontier models and companies.

## Host

- **Dwarkesh Patel** — Independent interviewer; rose to prominence with technically-grounded AI interviews; now one of the most-watched AI interviewers.

## Format

- **Category**: AI / long-form interview
- **Format**: Single guest, long-form
- **Cadence**: Weekly-ish
- **Episode length**: Often 2–4 hours
- **Language**: English

## Audience

AI researchers, founders, engineers, and serious followers of frontier-model progress; leans technical.

## Notable episodes

- Long interviews with CEOs/chiefs of major AI labs.
- Conversations with scientists on scaling, alignment, and capability frontiers.

## Reach & assessment

- Among the most-respected AI-native interview podcasts; huge YouTube presence and high clout in the AI research/engineering bubble.
- Business model: sponsorships + patronage.
